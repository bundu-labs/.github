## What we do

Bundu Labs is the engineering studio inside the Bundu Foundation. We
build the connective tissue across the ecosystem — the marketing
surfaces, internal tooling, and open-source repositories that the
public-facing brands (Nyuchi, Mukoko, …) sit on top of.

Engineering doctrine, design system, and Ubuntu-grounded constraints
are canonical at the
[Nyuchi org-wide repo](https://github.com/nyuchi/.github). What we
ship here follows the same rules.
